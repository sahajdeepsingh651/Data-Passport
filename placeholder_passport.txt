--- Decision record 1 (subject: job queue technology) ---
The engineering team evaluated the job queue technology for the notification
service and decided to use PostgreSQL LISTEN/NOTIFY instead of Redis. The
alternative considered was Redis Streams, which was rejected because the
persistence cost of running a dedicated Redis cluster with AOF durability
exceeded the value of its lower latency for this workload. The team also
considered RabbitMQ, rejected because it added an operational dependency
the platform did not already carry. This decision applies to the
notification, webhook-retry, and audit-log write paths, all of which share
the same durability requirements. It does not apply to the real-time
presence system, which has a different latency budget and remains on
Redis pub/sub. The decision was reviewed by the platform team and is
considered stable as of this quarter; any team building a new queue-backed
service should default to Postgres LISTEN/NOTIFY unless throughput
requirements exceed roughly five thousand messages per second, at which
point Kafka should be evaluated instead.
